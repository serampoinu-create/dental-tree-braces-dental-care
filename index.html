About us

Led by a specialist.
Run like a family clinic.

Dental Tree has been serving Imphal since 2015. We focus on orthodontics and general dental care — braces, aligners, root canals, extractions, and everything in between.

The clinic is led by Dr. N. Robinson Singh, who holds an MDS in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics and a BDS degree. That level of specialist training is uncommon in Manipur — it means your braces and bite correction are handled by someone who spent years studying exactly that.

We see patients of all ages, from children getting their first check-up to adults considering braces in their 30s. No pressure, no unnecessary procedures.
